Frequently Asked Questions

What is SEO and why is it important for my business?

▼
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your website to rank higher on search engines like Google. It's crucial for your business because most customers search for products/services online. With proper SEO, you can increase your visibility, attract qualified traffic, and boost your revenue. Studies show that 68% of clicks go to the top 3 results on Google.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

▼
SEO is a long-term strategy. Typically, you can start seeing initial results within 3-6 months, but more significant improvements usually appear after 6-12 months. The timeline depends on factors like competition level, current website state, keyword difficulty, and the aggressiveness of the SEO campaign. Patience and consistency are key to achieving lasting results.

What's the difference between On-Page and Off-Page SEO?

▼
On-Page SEO refers to optimizations done directly on your website, such as content quality, meta tags, header tags, and keyword optimization. Off-Page SEO involves activities outside your website, primarily building backlinks from other reputable sites. Both are essential for achieving top rankings. On-Page SEO controls your quality while Off-Page SEO builds your authority.

Can I do SEO myself or do I need a professional?

▼
While you can learn SEO basics and implement some strategies yourself, hiring a professional like Haris Altaf SEO provides significant advantages. A professional has years of experience, understands algorithm changes, can conduct competitive analysis, and dedicates full-time effort to your campaign. This typically results in faster, better, and more sustainable results compared to DIY efforts.

How do you measure SEO success?

▼
SEO success is measured through various metrics including keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, click-through rates, conversion rates, and ROI. We track your target keywords' positions on Google, monitor the increase in visitors from organic search, and measure how many visitors convert into customers. Regular reporting ensures you understand the progress and value of your SEO investment.
